Hi, I’m Unvirse

Somewhere out there is a free tool for exactly what you’re making.

Unvirse is a small shelf of them, picked by hand. Scroll and I’ll explain.

The internet has thousands of tools. Most aren’t for you.

You know the feeling. Every search ends up here:

So I read the fine print for you.

Three rules to get on the shelf.

  • Free to use.

    If it needs a card on file, it stays out.

  • Open source first.

    You can read the code, so the tool can’t quietly change on you.

  • Described honestly.

    Plain words about what it does, and what it doesn’t.

Made by people all over the world.

Each pin is a tool, placed where its team has roots. Community projects with no single home stay off the map.
